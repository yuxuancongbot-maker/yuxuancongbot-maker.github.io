I am a senior undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at Dalian University of Technology (ranked 2nd out of 64). My research interests lie at the intersection of embodied AI, robot learning, and video world models.

I am particularly interested in physically consistent world models for robotic interaction and efficient generative policies for real-time, closed-loop robotic systems. My research experience includes work with Prof. Guohao Dai’s team at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and an engineering internship at Infinigence AI.

Selected Publications

STEP: Warm-Started Visuomotor Policies with Spatiotemporal Consistency Prediction

Jinhao Li, Yuxuan Cong, Yingqiao Wang, Hao Xia, Shan Huang, Yijia Zhang, Ningyi Xu, Guohao Dai
ICML 2026 · Equal contribution (second-listed author)

A spatiotemporal warm-start framework for diffusion policies that reduces denoising from 100 steps to 2, with a lightweight Transformer predictor and velocity-aware noise injection. Evaluated on simulation benchmarks and a physical SO-ARM101 robot.

Selected Projects

APXInf: Edge Inference for Embodied AI

Open-source project · 2026

An open-source inference engine jointly developed by Infinigence AI, Tsinghua University, and Shanghai Jiao Tong University for real-time VLA inference on edge devices. The framework achieves under 26 ms end-to-end latency for π0.5 FP8 on Jetson Thor (approximately 10.7× speedup over 278 ms).

My contributions include discrepancy-aware dynamic flow-matching step scheduling, cross-frame KV cache reuse, and closed-loop deployment and performance validation on a Franka robot.

Vision-Guided Autonomous Underwater Robot

China Robot Competition · National First Prize

An autonomous underwater robotic system integrating vision perception, task planning, and low-level control on ArduPilot and NVIDIA Jetson. I led control algorithm development, including YOLOv8-pose-based perception, finite-state-machine task scheduling, and pool testing.
